How it works

From chat to plan in three steps

  1. Pick your hobbies

    Hiking, padel, cinema, coffee… Each hobby is a chat with people from your province and age range.

  2. Chat in your chats

    Reply in threads to keep things tidy, mention anyone and react without flooding the chat.

  3. Plan it in a group

    Create a private group, invite whoever is in and sort out cars, times and photos without the noise.

Features

Made for chatting with lots of people without getting lost

Big chats stay readable, groups are yours and direct messages arrive instantly.

Local chats

Many voices at once, all readable

Every topic gets its own thread and the main conversation stays clean. Catch up on what you missed without endless scrolling.

  • Threads with a summary of replies and participants
  • Mentions with their own inbox, and alerts only when someone names you
  • A new-messages divider and a button to jump to the latest
  • Reactions, quoted replies and pinned messages

FAQ

What people usually ask

How much does it cost?

Nothing. MeetGo is free: create your account and use it without limits, no card needed.

How much does it cost?

€2.50 a month or €25 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Both options include everything.

How do I get the free year?

Create your account with the code LANZAMIENTO while places last. It is only for the first 50 accounts and we don't ask for a card.

What happens when the free year ends?

Your account becomes read-only until you subscribe: you can read your conversations but not write. Nothing is deleted.

Who sees my phone number?

Nobody. Your number is not on your profile, and messages sharing numbers in chats are removed automatically.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes, MeetGo is for people over 18. Chats are grouped by age range: 18-30, 31-60 and over 60.

Which cities does it work in?

Every province in Spain. Your chats are those of your province and age range, one for each hobby you choose.

How do I cancel?

From your MeetGo account if you pay by card, or from Google Play if you subscribed in the app. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

Is there an app for Android and iPhone?

On Android, download the app. On iPhone, open meetgo.app/app in Safari and add it to your Home Screen.
